# Intelligence in Service of Life — release direction

## Experience thesis

The recipient is a builder, creator, educator, parent, or public-interest leader who already senses that advanced intelligence changes the distribution of power. The prior state is abstract agreement with “AI for everyone” without a usable standard. The observed truth is that access alone does not preserve agency. The proposition is that a constitution can become a product operating system. The consequence is a portfolio that can be examined through concrete tests: authorship, portability, contestability, capability gained, and stewardship.

## Three directions considered

1. **Prismatic editorial accord — selected.** A warm civic document with one incoming signal refracted through a constitutional mark into distinct venture lines. It gives the doctrine gravity without imitating a government site and lets each venture retain its own expression.
2. **Protocol ledger.** A denser, technical specification with versioned clauses, matrices, and compliance status above the fold. Strong for governance audiences; too cold as the first public encounter.
3. **Living futures cinema.** Full-bleed world imagery and scroll choreography showing possible futures. Emotionally expansive; too dependent on imagery and less credible as a constitutional foundation.

The selected direction uses one earned motion: the prism signal draws once on entry. Reading, navigation, and principle tests remain still. Reduced motion shows the complete composition immediately.

Starlight owns the doctrine. FrankX interprets it under a named human voice. GenCreator operationalizes creator agency. Arcanea operationalizes imagination and cultural possibility. The pages share claims and links, never duplicate bodies of copy.
